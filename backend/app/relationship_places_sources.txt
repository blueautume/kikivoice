Input: CN.zip, HK.zip, MO.zip, TW.zip, downloaded on 2026-11-03.
GeoNames supplies WGS84 representative points for administrative regions,
not exact birth or street addresses. This catalog extracts a subset, matches
Mainland names and parent codes, and reorganizes the display hierarchy.
The GeoNames record ID is preserved alongside each point. No coordinates are
inferred from nearby places when the administrative record cannot be matched.

Hong Kong covers all 18 districts. Macao covers seven parishes and Cotai,
shown as a selectable area. Taiwan covers 22 counties/cities and 368
townships, county-administered cities and districts.
Hong Kong labels were checked against the Digital Policy Office:
https://www.digitalpolicy.gov.hk/en/our_work/data_governance/policies_standards/interoperability_framework/code_lists/district_council_code_chi/index.html
Macao parish labels were checked against the cadastral map:
https://cadastre.gis.gov.mo/MGSP_Cad/chn/bak/main20150917.html

Reviewed coordinate supplements (2026-11-06)
The original 3,633-entry hierarchy and all 3,368 existing coordinates are preserved.
Scripts/Data/china-coordinate-supplements.json adds 265 source-linked WGS84
representative points: 258 selectable leaf areas and seven city-level entries.
All 3,633 catalog entries, including all 3,248 leaves, now have offline coordinates.
This does not update the dated administrative hierarchy or add street addresses.

158 points use administrative records in the pinned GeoNames CN.zip. Identity is
checked through the exact Chinese division code on a Wikidata entity (P442) and
that entity's explicit GeoNames identifier (P1566), with the province and feature
level also checked. Some GeoNames labels retain a former county/district name.
These links establish entity identity, not an independent surveying verification.

Two geographic island-group records (GeoNames 1821073 and 1821005, feature ISLS)
come from no-country.zip, downloaded on 2026-11-06. They represent the Xisha and
Nansha island groups named by the fixed catalog, not single islands or newer
administrative districts. no-country.zip is covered by the same GeoNames CC BY 4.0
license. The absence of an ISO country code in that source is not used to change
the catalog's hierarchy or regional assignment.

105 points use non-deprecated Earth coordinate statements (P625) from Wikidata,
retrieved on 2026-11-06. Wikidata structured data is CC0; Earth coordinates use
WGS84. The supplement records each entity revision, original coordinate statement
ID, declared precision when available, and the exact code/name/parent identity.
Multiple coordinate statements are reviewed individually, never averaged. Most
are matched by the exact P442 division code. The Zhongsha geographic island group
(Q197893) is matched by the fixed catalog's geographic name, not by substituting
Macclesfield Bank, a single island, or a newer administrative district.
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing

https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P442
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P1566

Every point is an administrative-area, administrative-seat or geographic-area
representative, not an exact birth location. The three island groups span large
sea areas. Some source precision is coarse (for example Tongguan District,
340705, has a declared Wikidata precision of about 0.3141 degrees). Precision and
selection notes are retained in the supplement; printed decimal places are not
a guarantee of surveying accuracy. No nearby city or province coordinate was
copied into a missing district as a fallback.

Rebuild: python3 Scripts/generate_china_districts.py <directory with inputs>
The generator also reads the tracked Scripts/Data/china-coordinate-supplements.json
by default, validates each complete district path, and refuses to overwrite an
upstream point. Rebuilding requires no network access. To check data integrity
and byte-for-byte reproducibility with the pinned input directory:
BLINDORACLE_DISTRICT_SOURCE_DIR=<directory with inputs> python3 -m unittest Scripts.tests.test_china_district_coordinates
